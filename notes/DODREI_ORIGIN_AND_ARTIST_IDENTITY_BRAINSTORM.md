# DODREI — Origin / Memory / Fiction / Artist Identity Brainstorm

Status: **raw private-origin brainstorm / not a public artist statement**  
Captured: 2026-08-27  
Purpose: preserve autobiographical origin, conceptual associations, fictional cover-myths, research directions, and possible future artist-identity language **before refinement**. Contradictions are allowed. Nothing here should be treated as final canon.

---

## 0. Why this note exists

DODREI did not begin as a neutral software project or as a formal experiment in image processing. Its deeper origin is autobiographical: a relationship, a shared period of image-making, the difficulty of remembering it, and the attempt to keep material that could not simply be discarded.

At the same time, there is a strong desire **not to disclose that origin literally**. The work may retain traces, clues, misdirections, false documents, invented etymologies, fictional species, and alternate explanations. Ideally the public-facing fiction should not merely conceal the private story; it should become an independent conceptual structure that is interesting even when the original story is unknown.

The central possibility is therefore:

> **Hide truth inside fiction, then let the fiction acquire enough density that it becomes a second truth.**

Or:

> Put a more factual-looking falsehood on top of the fact.

The concealment itself is not merely defensive. It may become part of the artistic method.

# 1. Autobiographical origin — raw account

The person at the origin of this body of work is **Audrey**.

I loved Audrey. A significant part of my later media-art practice began while missing her and trying to live with the residue of that relationship.

We first began making images together. I was filming; she was the model. The recordings were not initially made as a tightly planned artwork. Much of it was closer to documentation: fragments, moments, images made because we were together and making things together.

We were muses to each other.

During that process, a friendship developed into an ambiguous and powerful romantic feeling. That shift made the relationship increasingly difficult. We both tried to preserve the relationship, but eventually we separated.

Afterward, Audrey continued her own artistic practice, including media-art work. My own trajectory changed as well. I had lived primarily through music, but gradually moved into video, interactive media, and media art. In that sense, the relationship did not merely leave memories or footage; it altered the direction of my artistic life.

The clips we made together were too beautiful for me to discard.

They were not necessarily designed to become a finished work. Precisely because they were ordinary documentation, however, they had a strange intensity. I began editing them together. At some point I no longer wanted the footage to behave like a conventional documentary or chronological recollection. I wanted it to move as memory actually felt to me: compressed, layered, random, interrupted, excessive, repetitive, partially inaccessible, and emotionally overdetermined.

The wish was not simply to remember.

Remembering was painful.

There was simultaneously:

- the desire to preserve;
- the desire to destroy or distort;
- the desire to be recognized by her;
- the desire to hide the reference from her;
- the desire to say her name;
- the desire to make the name unreadable;
- longing;
- shame or embarrassment about longing;
- gratitude;
- regret;
- an inability to assign a clean ending or causal explanation.

This contradiction is important. It may be one of the genuine origins of the later work.

# 2. Name genealogy

The name did not appear fully formed. It mutated.

A rough genealogy is:

```text
AUDREY
  ↓
NAUDREI
  ↓
DAUDREI
  ↓
DODREI
```

There were also adjacent possibilities such as `DOUDREI`, `NODREI`, `AUDERYN`, and `ADERYN`, but **DODREI / Dodrei / 도드레이** is currently preferred.

## 2.1 NAUDREI

`Naudrei` may have contained an early defensive phrase:

- **No Audrey**
- **Never Audrey**

This fits the emotional contradiction of the period. The name simultaneously kept Audrey inside itself and attempted to negate her.

`Naudrei` may therefore be understood as an impossible instruction:

> Do not remember the person whose name has already been embedded in the instruction.

There was also a private hope that perhaps one day she might see the name and wonder whether it referred to her, combined with an equally strong wish that she would not know.

## 2.2 DAUDREI

The N later became D. The exact semantic reason need not be reconstructed as if it had been planned from the beginning. The mutation itself is useful: memory often acquires explanations only afterward.

Possible retrospective readings of D include:

- **Dear** — affection after the defensive `No / Never` has softened;
- **Distance** — temporal, physical, emotional, representational distance;
- **Document** — the documentary footage at the origin of the work;
- **Double** — the remembered person as a second, reconstructed version of the actual person;
- **Drift** — memory drifting away from its source;
- **Desire** — attraction, lack, and projection;
- **Departure** — separation without needing to make departure the sole meaning;
- **Dream** — useful but perhaps too obvious;
- **Dove** — possible bird association, though this should not become forced symbolism.

The strongest cluster currently feels like:

> **Dear / Distance / Document / Double / Drift**

D does not have to be a single acronym. It may be an unstable letter whose meanings accumulate retrospectively.

## 2.3 DODREI

`Dodrei` is far enough from `Audrey` to function independently, but still feels like a damaged or displaced residue of a name.

This is potentially more important than any invented dictionary definition.

> **DODREI is a name left after a name has been remembered for too long.**

Another useful formulation:

> **DODREI is what remained after I stopped trying to remember her correctly.**

At this stage Audrey need no longer be the subject of the work. She can remain its origin.

That distinction is crucial:

```text
subject ≠ origin
```

Future DODREI works do not need to contain Audrey's image, story, or identity. The work may instead preserve the altered way of looking that emerged from that period.

# 3. From a person to a way of seeing

A possible trajectory:

```text
Audrey
  ↓
filming together
  ↓
relationship / mutual muse
  ↓
separation
  ↓
unresolved footage
  ↓
editing as recollection
  ↓
recollection as distortion
  ↓
distortion as visual language
  ↓
DODREI
```

Earlier, the camera pointed toward a person and the landscape existed around her.

Now the person is absent and the camera continues to point at the world.

Recent images increasingly contain:

- empty or nearly empty landscapes;
- loneliness;
- uncanny quiet;
- places that appear to have just been vacated;
- ordinary locations that acquire emotional density without an explicit event;
- surfaces, lights, rooms, roads, plants, water, windows, streets, architecture;
- images in which the absence of a person becomes more important than a visible figure.

One useful conceptual sentence:

> **A camera that once filmed someone continues filming the world after that person is gone.**

The later landscapes can therefore be read as **afterimages without the original figure**.

This does not mean every landscape is secretly “about Audrey.” That would narrow the work too much. Rather, the experience may have produced a durable perceptual habit: sensitivity to absence, trace, temporal residue, and the possibility that an ordinary place carries an event that cannot be seen.

# 4. Current philosophical field: memory

A major current research direction is the nature of memory itself, especially where memory stops being a reliable archive and becomes an active generative system.

Important concepts to expand later:

## 4.1 Original incompleteness

Memory is not a complete recording that later decays. It may be **incomplete from the moment of formation**.

Questions:

- Is there ever a complete original memory?
- If perception itself is selective, is “loss” the wrong model?
- Does memory begin as compression rather than documentation?
- Is the first remembered version already an edit?

This matters because the work should not always imply a perfect image that is subsequently corrupted by glitch. A more radical model is:

> There was never a complete image to begin with.

That could affect visual architecture, content pools, interaction, audio, and narrative structure.

## 4.2 Randomness

Randomness should not be treated merely as a visual-effect generator.

It may model the fact that recollection is contingent:

- one image arrives instead of another;
- one detail becomes vivid for no obvious reason;
- repetition is uneven;
- emotionally trivial fragments survive while important information disappears;
- accidental adjacency creates apparent meaning.

Randomness can therefore be **epistemological**, not decorative.

The system can ask:

> If two fragments repeatedly appear next to each other, when does the viewer begin to believe that they belong together?

## 4.3 Retrospective meaning

Meaning is often assigned after the event.

The present reorganizes the past.

A later event can make an earlier trivial gesture appear prophetic, causal, romantic, ominous, or inevitable even when it was none of those things at the time.

Possible artistic principle:

> **Causality can be generated by editing.**

Or more broadly:

> Sequence creates a story, and the story then pretends the sequence was inevitable.

This can be implemented formally through random ordering, persistent state, false recurrence, delayed captions, contradictory metadata, or later scenes that retroactively alter how earlier scenes are interpreted.

## 4.4 Multiple / plural truths

A memory need not have a single authoritative version.

Possible models:

- two incompatible memories can both remain emotionally true;
- two people can remember the same event differently;
- the same person can remember it differently at different times;
- a false detail may carry a truer emotional structure than a factual detail;
- factual accuracy and experiential truth are not identical.

This suggests a **plural memory architecture** rather than a canonical timeline.

A memory node might hold:

```text
memory A1
memory A2
memory A3
```

where A1/A2/A3 are mutually inconsistent, and the system refuses to choose a winner.

## 4.5 Memory as reconstruction

Each recall may rewrite the memory being recalled.

The work could therefore avoid treating memory as a file retrieved from storage. Recall itself becomes transformation.

Potential system principle:

```text
recall(memory_n)
    -> produces memory_n+1
```

Repeated access does not reveal the original. It creates descendants.

## 4.6 Observer-generated object

A useful fictional/scientific formulation from the Dodrei bird idea:

> **The defining characteristics of Dodrei may occur in the observer rather than in the specimen.**

Applied to memory:

> The remembered object cannot be separated cleanly from the person remembering it.

This may become one of the most productive conceptual links between personal history and system design.

# 5. DODREI as fictional bird — cover mythology / alternate truth

A strong fictional frame is to claim that **Dodrei is the name of a fictional bird**.

This can function simultaneously as:

1. concealment of the autobiographical origin;
2. an independent myth;
3. an allegory of memory;
4. a generative world-building device;
5. a source of false archives, field notes, taxonomies, sound, text, and interaction rules.

## 5.1 Basic myth

Dodrei is a small migratory bird whose existence is uncertain.

It is unusual because direct sightings are rare. Most evidence appears in:

- old photographs;
- incomplete video;
- audio recordings;
- damaged field notes;
- secondary testimony;
- locations where the bird is said to have appeared, despite no bird being visible.

Researchers gradually stop collecting images of the bird itself and begin collecting **traces of places where it may have been**.

This elegantly maps onto the move from filming a person to filming empty landscapes.

## 5.2 Pair behavior

Dodrei may be described as usually travelling in pairs.

The pair gradually learns to imitate each other's calls. After sufficient time together, it becomes difficult to determine which sound originally belonged to which bird.

Then, in most surviving records, one bird disappears.

The remaining bird:

- revisits earlier routes;
- returns to empty places;
- repeats old calls;
- imitates the absent bird;
- gradually changes the imitation;
- eventually produces a call that may never have belonged to either bird.

This transformed call can itself be called **Dodrei**.

Thus the name may ambiguously refer to:

- the species;
- one bird;
- the pair;
- the absent bird;
- the remaining bird;
- the call;
- the corrupted imitation;
- the archive;
- the observer's condition.

This ambiguity is desirable.

A useful line:

> **Dodrei is not necessarily the bird. It may be the sound produced after one bird has remembered another for too long.**

## 5.3 Contradictory descriptions

Reports should disagree.

One observer records gray feathers. Another says black. Another records red markings. One video seems to show two birds; another implies there was only one. Later testimony by the same observer contradicts earlier notes.

Do not resolve the contradiction.

This makes the fictional ornithology a model of plural memory.

## 5.4 Places where Dodrei appears

Rather than spectacular wilderness, sightings may occur in emotionally ambiguous ordinary environments:

- empty streets;
- riverbanks;
- apartment windows;
- roadside trees;
- parking lots at night;
- rooms after someone leaves;
- abandoned or merely unused structures;
- artificial light on leaves;
- train platforms;
- quiet industrial areas;
- ordinary landscapes that appear to contain a missing event.

Late Dodrei records may contain no birds at all.

Only landscape remains.

## 5.5 Voice / human-name clue

A subtle clue can be left in fictional ornithological literature:

> The call is sometimes mistaken for a human voice, particularly a short female name.

Do **not** specify the name.

This becomes a private trace back toward Audrey without revealing the mechanism.

## 5.6 Unstable spelling and pronunciation

Fictional archive sources can disagree on spelling:

```text
Dodrei
Dodri
Dodrey
D'odrei
```

Likewise pronunciation can fluctuate between **도드리** and **도드레이**.

This can absorb a real aesthetic preference into the fiction rather than forcing one phonetic authority.

## 5.7 ADERYN

`Aderyn` remains an interesting adjacent term. It has strong visual and semantic usefulness because of its association with “bird” in Welsh and its faint structural resemblance to `AUDREY`.

Potential fictional uses:

- obsolete field name for Dodrei;
- early taxonomic term;
- name of a disputed specimen;
- name found in an old notebook;
- a researcher / witness / location rather than the bird itself;
- a false etymological ancestor of `Dodrei`.

Possible hidden chain:

```text
AUDREY
   ↓
ADERYN
   ↓
DODREI
```

Person → bird → trace → artwork.

This should remain optional. It may be too elegant if overexplained.

# 6. Truth, falsehood, and Borges

There is a strong affinity with **Jorge Luis Borges**, not necessarily because the work should imitate his surface style, but because of a shared attraction to structures in which fiction is given the documentary weight of fact.

Important Borges-related directions to research and discuss later:

- invented books and authors;
- false citations;
- imaginary encyclopedias;
- archives that create the world they claim merely to describe;
- labyrinths;
- doubles;
- mirrors;
- infinite regress;
- competing versions of history;
- the map replacing the territory;
- systems whose internal consistency becomes more persuasive than external reality;
- philosophical concepts embodied as fictional objects rather than explained as essays.

The attraction here is not simply “surrealism.” A more precise field may include **fantastic literature, metaphysical fiction, metafiction, pseudo-scholarship, invented documentary apparatus, and ontological uncertainty**. This terminology should be researched more carefully later.

The artistic impulse can be stated as:

> I like hiding truth inside a lie.

> I like turning truth into a lie by describing it too precisely in the wrong language.

> I like placing a false account over an actual event until the false account appears more factual than the event itself.

> I like a document that seems to clarify something while actually making its origin harder to locate.

This should not become an ARG whose purpose is merely “solve the secret.” The hidden truth need not be recoverable. The clues can exist without guaranteeing a solution.

The fiction is not a lockbox containing a single answer.

Ideally:

```text
private fact
    ↓ transformation
fictional fact
    ↓ accumulation
independent world
```

At the end, the fictional world should have its own truth-value.

# 7. Antonio Carlos Jobim as background / artistic atmosphere

Antonio Carlos Jobim is an important musical and emotional reference and may become part of the broader background of the work and artist identity.

This section is currently a **research agenda**, not a finalized factual essay. Future work should verify details carefully rather than rely on remembered anecdotes.

Areas to investigate deeply:

## 7.1 Life and biography

- Jobim's life trajectory;
- Rio de Janeiro and the environments in which he lived/worked;
- relationships with poets, singers, musicians, arrangers, filmmakers;
- anecdotes revealing temperament and worldview;
- relationship to nature and birds;
- how later life differed emotionally and musically from early bossa nova mythology.

## 7.2 Historical and Brazilian context

- pre-bossa Brazilian popular music;
- samba, samba-canção, choro, and other relevant lineages;
- the emergence of bossa nova;
- postwar modernization of Brazil;
- Rio as both real city and constructed cultural image;
- political and social changes surrounding Jobim's career;
- relationship between Brazilian music and U.S. jazz;
- export, exoticization, international reception, and the difference between Brazilian self-understanding and foreign fantasy.

## 7.3 Musical language

Topics to analyze technically:

- harmonic movement;
- voice leading;
- chromatic inner lines;
- major/minor ambiguity;
- extended harmony;
- tonicization and modulation;
- bass motion;
- repeated harmonic cells;
- melody against harmony;
- rhythmic restraint;
- orchestration;
- use of silence and space;
- relationship between apparently simple surfaces and harmonically complex interiors.

A useful question for DODREI:

> Can visual systems behave like Jobim harmony — emotionally clear on the surface while structurally ambiguous underneath?

Another:

> Can an image remain quiet while its internal causal structure continuously shifts?

## 7.4 Lyrics / saudade / emotional ambiguity

Study lyrics associated with Jobim and collaborators with attention to:

- longing;
- absence;
- return;
- memory;
- nature;
- sea, rain, birds, forests, streets, seasons;
- tenderness without sentimental overstatement;
- the coexistence of beauty and loss;
- **saudade** without reducing it to a cliché or one-word translation.

Potential relevance: DODREI should not necessarily become dark, tragic, or gothic simply because its origin involves loss. Jobim may offer another emotional model: **beauty that does not cancel sadness, and sadness that does not require ugliness**.

## 7.5 Birds

The user's observation that Jobim used bird names / bird imagery in titles is especially interesting and should be researched systematically.

Future research should catalogue:

- compositions directly named after birds;
- bird calls / nature references;
- titles whose bird associations may be obscured in translation;
- ecological/natural imagery in lyrics and interviews;
- whether birds function as literal natural subjects, Brazilian identity, freedom, observation, sound, or something else.

This is a potentially powerful bridge:

```text
Jobim / birds / nature / song
             ↕
Dodrei / fictional bird / memory / image
```

The connection should remain subtle. Avoid making DODREI “a Jobim-inspired bird project” unless future work genuinely supports that direction.

# 8. Possible artist-identity field

A possible long-term artistic identity is emerging, but it should remain open rather than turned prematurely into a manifesto.

Recurring interests currently include:

- memory as reconstruction rather than storage;
- absence as an active visual presence;
- records that outlive their original purpose;
- documentary material becoming fiction;
- fiction acquiring documentary authority;
- random systems generating apparent causality;
- meaning assigned retrospectively;
- mutually incompatible versions remaining valid;
- ordinary landscapes carrying invisible events;
- sound and image as traces rather than representations;
- the gap between factual truth and emotional truth;
- intimate biography encrypted into impersonal systems;
- interfaces that behave like unstable recollection;
- a musician's sense of time, repetition, harmony, improvisation, and variation entering visual work.

A provisional formulation:

> The work does not try to recover the past accurately. It constructs systems in which fragments repeatedly acquire, lose, and exchange meaning.

Another:

> The archive is treated not as evidence of what happened, but as material from which new causes and memories can be manufactured.

Another:

> The missing subject remains present through the behavior of the system that learned to look for it.

# 9. Formal strategies that could grow from these ideas

These are brainstorming seeds, not implementation requests.

## 9.1 False metadata

A real image may receive fictional metadata. A fictional image may receive precise documentary metadata. Metadata can disagree between views.

Possible fields:

- specimen number;
- observer;
- confidence percentage;
- location;
- weather;
- capture time;
- contradictory date;
- “reconstructed” flag;
- archive source;
- missing file reference;
- uncertain taxonomy.

The precision of metadata should increase rather than decrease uncertainty.

## 9.2 Multiple versions of one memory

One source image could generate several incompatible “memory records,” each with different text, date, crop, color, sound, or causal relation.

No version is marked canonical.

## 9.3 Retrospective causality

An event occurring later can modify metadata or interpretation of an earlier scene.

Example:

- first visit: image appears meaningless;
- later encounter: a text fragment appears;
- returning to the first image now reveals new metadata;
- viewer feels the first image “always meant” something it did not originally contain.

## 9.4 Random recurrence mistaken for fate

A random image may recur several times by chance. The system does not prevent repetition. The viewer may interpret recurrence as intention.

This is especially relevant because DODREI's current visual scene selection deliberately allows repeats and duplicates.

The system can exploit the human tendency to invent causality from recurrence without secretly scripting every recurrence.

## 9.5 False conservation

The viewer may believe an image is being preserved, while each recall actually mutates it.

## 9.6 Archive contamination

Memory A can gradually acquire fragments originally associated with B or C.

After sufficient time, provenance becomes impossible to determine.

## 9.7 Absent subject detection

A system could repeatedly “detect” a subject that is not visibly present:

```text
subject confidence 0.63
subject confidence 0.71
subject confidence 0.12
```

Never reveal whether this is an algorithmic error, fictional bird detection, or emotional projection.

## 9.8 Sound as unreliable witness

Audio may imply an event not visible in the image. Later the sound may attach itself to another image, making the viewer uncertain where it belonged.

## 9.9 Field-guide fiction

A DODREI field guide could gradually emerge:

- plates;
- maps;
- calls;
- sightings;
- disputed specimens;
- extinct subspecies;
- observer notes;
- contradictory classifications.

The guide could be aesthetically dry rather than fantastical, making the lie appear factual.

## 9.10 The bird disappears from the work

An important long-term rule may be that the fictional bird itself is rarely or never shown.

Eventually only its supposed environment remains.

This parallels the autobiographical movement while remaining a complete fiction on its own.

# 10. Avoid over-explanation

A major risk is converting a rich private origin into a neat symbolic dictionary.

Avoid:

```text
D = Dear
O = ...
D = Distance
R = Recall
E = ...
I = ...
```

unless used deliberately as an obviously dubious or fictional interpretation.

The name is stronger if its meanings are **sedimentary** rather than encoded one letter at a time.

Likewise, the fictional Dodrei bird should not become a conventional fantasy creature with fixed lore, anatomy, powers, and plot.

Better:

- incomplete records;
- uncertain descriptions;
- multiple explanations;
- mundane details;
- gaps;
- contradictions;
- suspiciously precise facts;
- no definitive reveal.

The fiction should behave like memory itself.

# 14. Provisional author-identity sketch

Do not finalize yet.

A possible emerging identity is an artist who began with music and came to moving image / interactive media through an intimate act of documentation. The work increasingly treats media not as transparent records but as unstable machines for generating recollection.

The artist is interested in what happens when:

- documentation loses its original purpose;
- random juxtaposition begins to look intentional;
- viewers create causes after seeing effects;
- absence becomes more visible than presence;
- factual records become fiction;
- fictional records become more persuasive than factual memory;
- a private emotional origin is translated into impersonal systems;
- musical thinking — time, repetition, improvisation, harmony, variation — migrates into visual and interactive structures.

This identity should not be reduced to “an artist making work about a lost love.” The autobiographical event is an origin point, not the final subject.

A stronger long-term field may be:

> **memory, absence, unstable documentation, constructed causality, plural truth, and systems that generate meaning after the fact.**

DODREI can be one of the first names given to that field.

# 15. Final note for future refinement

Do not clean this history too aggressively.

The fact that `NAUDREI`, `DAUDREI`, and `DODREI` acquired meaning **after** their creation is itself consistent with the work's conceptual interest in retrospective causality.

It may therefore be a mistake to invent a single perfect origin story and pretend it was always intended.

Keep both levels:

```text
what actually happened
what I later believed it meant
what I invented to conceal it
what the invented story later became
```

Those four layers do not need to agree.

Their disagreement may be the work.
